## The appeal is the setup, not the label

A stepmom or stepsister scenario comes with built-in tension: two people who are supposed to keep a certain distance, and a plausible reason the scene is happening anyway. That framing does a lot of work — it gives a scene a beginning, a reason, and stakes, without needing an actual script. Compare that to a scene with no setup at all, and it's easy to see why taboo-roleplay content consistently outperforms generic categories.

## Stepmom vs stepsister — different dynamics

The two aren't interchangeable:

- **Stepsister scenes** lean younger, more playful, often framed around "getting caught" or shared space (a shared room, a shared bed).
- **Stepmom scenes** lean into an authority/experience dynamic — the MILF-adjacent angle, often with the stepmom character initiating.
